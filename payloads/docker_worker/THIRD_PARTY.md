# Runtime dependencies

The container uses NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.2.0 under the
[NVIDIA Omniverse license](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-omniverse-license-agreement/).
The workspace uses [websockets 15.0.1](https://github.com/python-websockets/websockets/tree/15.0.1)
under the BSD 3-Clause license. Isaac Sim supplies Python, Pillow and the
OpenUSD and rendering libraries used by the simulation process.

The original USD model takes visual cues from Michael Raspuzzi's Andrew+
illustration. [3D Lab Views](https://michaelraspuzzi.github.io/labautomationin3d/#andrew-plus)

The workcell also includes Open Lab Automata's MK3 frame mesh and QUIKTECH's
Erlenmeyer flask mesh. Their license and source details ship with the assets.
[OpenUSD asset notices](../isaac/assets/vendor/NOTICE.txt)
